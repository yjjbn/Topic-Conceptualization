]

* ID: ID of the text document
* Label: Your classification, 1 or 0
* Explanation: An explanation of the rationale for your classification. Explain which parts of the codebook's clarification or negative clarification you relied on the make your decision. If you faced any ambiguities or difficulties in making a classification, you can describe that here.
